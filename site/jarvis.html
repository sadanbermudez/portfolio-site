How it works

all free

The same Jarvis, minus the hands

What he knows
The text of this website, and nothing else. Each question searches its pages (BM25), and the best passages go to the model, which must cite them.
The brain
Qwen3 30B, the same family as the model my local Jarvis runs, on Cloudflare Workers AI's free daily allowance. When it runs out, Jarvis says so until midnight UTC.
The ears
Your recording goes to Whisper large-v3-turbo on Cloudflare and comes back as text. Nothing is stored.
The voice
Kokoro-82M with the Daniel voice, running in your browser in a Web Worker. No server speaks for him.
What he cannot do
Read my files, email or calendar, run commands, or browse. At home Jarvis has tools on my laptop; this edition has none.
Measured
Graded against 33 written questions with fixed checks. Answers stream in under a second. On questions it had not seen during building, it answered 5 to 7 of 8 fully, with a citation, across three runs; the rest were a missing citation or an honest "I don't know", with no false statements in the final runs. The voice starts 2 to 6 seconds after the text on a fast laptop, and later on slower devices. It can still be wrong: the numbered links show where each answer came from.
Guard rails
A few questions a minute per visitor, short answers, a fixed persona that declines private or unrelated requests, and a strict security policy on every page.
