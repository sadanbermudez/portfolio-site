In plain words
This is a public version of my assistant, Jarvis. Ask about my work by typing or by voice, and he answers from the text of this website, with numbered links showing where each answer came from. He cannot see anything private.
Why it matters
A recruiter can question the site instead of reading all of it, and the Fit check below compares a job description with what the site shows. Answering only from sources, and saying "I don't know" otherwise, is what makes an assistant worth trusting.
What I built
Search over this site's pages by words and by meaning, a hosted model that must cite the passages it uses, speech to text, a voice chosen to suit each device, rate limits and a fixed persona, and a set of graded questions.
What I measured
Graded against 33 written questions with fixed checks. On questions it had not seen during building, it answered 5 to 7 of 8 fully, with a citation, across three runs, with no false statements in the final runs. Answers start to stream in under a second.
Glossary: the technical words on this page
Language model
The AI that writes the answers: here Gemma 4 26B, hosted on Cloudflare.
Retrieval
Finding the passages that answer a question before the model writes anything.
BM25
A classic search method that ranks passages by the words they share with the question.
Embeddings
Numbers that capture what a passage means, so a search can match meaning rather than exact words.
Citation
A numbered link to the passage an answer came from, so you can check it.
Speech to text
Whisper large-v3-turbo turns your recording into text.
Text to speech
Kokoro, the device's own voice or MeloTTS, depending on the device, turns the answer into speech.
Workers AI
Cloudflare's service for running AI models, used here on its free daily allowance.

How it works

all free

The same Jarvis, minus the hands

What he knows
The text of this website, and nothing else. Each question searches its pages by words (BM25) and by meaning (embeddings), and the best passages go to the model, which must cite them.
The brain
Gemma 4 26B on Cloudflare Workers AI's free daily allowance, chosen over Qwen3 30B (the family my local Jarvis runs) because it cited its sources and answered more completely in testing. When it runs out, Jarvis says so until midnight UTC.
The ears
Your recording goes to Whisper large-v3-turbo on Cloudflare and comes back as text. Nothing is stored.
The voice
Kokoro-82M with the Daniel voice, running in your browser, when the page measures that your computer makes speech faster than he speaks. On a phone that would take minutes, so he uses the device's own British voice if it has one, or MeloTTS on Cloudflare, one sentence at a time. If a voice is late, the next one takes over.
What he cannot do
Read my files, email or calendar, run commands, or browse. At home Jarvis has tools on my laptop; this edition has none.
Measured
Graded against 33 written questions with fixed checks. Answers stream in under a second. On questions it had not seen during building, it answered 5 to 7 of 8 fully, with a citation, across three runs; the rest were a missing citation or an honest "I don't know", with no false statements in the final runs. On a slowed-down phone over 4G, the voice started 1.9 to 2.5 seconds after the text, or 0.7 seconds with the phone's own voice. It can still be wrong: the numbered links show where each answer came from.
Guard rails
A few questions a minute per visitor, short answers, a fixed persona that declines private or unrelated requests, and a strict security policy on every page.
