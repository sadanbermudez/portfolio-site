My story

Six cities, four fields, one chessboard

I have always loved science and technology. This is where they took me.

01 · MADRID · WHERE I GREW UP

A chessboard at four, biochemistry at university

BSc in Biochemistry at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (2010 to 2014), in nanomedicine and molecular biology. My first paper came from it.

02 · COPENHAGEN

Nanoscience, with a major in photophysics

MSc at the University of Copenhagen, and my first machine-learning code: Python and R, for bioinformatics.

03 · GLASGOW

From scientist to engineer

A Centre for Doctoral Training in Intelligent Sensing and Measurement (Glasgow and Edinburgh), starting with a year of MSc in Engineering applied to photonics.

04 · EDINBURGH

A PhD in light, oxygen and fibres

Detecting singlet oxygen, the molecule that kills cells in photodynamic therapy, made by two-photon excitation inside hollow-core photonic crystal fibres. It took seven versions of the experiment.

Leadership Award (University of Edinburgh, 2019) · talks at the European Society for Photobiology · Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light autumn academy

The research, interactively →

05 · WIESBADEN

A short stop in Germany

Two months between the PhD and the postdoc, analysing my PhD results and looking for postdoc positions.

06 · BARCELONA

Research, then industry, then AI

Two years as a postdoc at IQS (Universitat Ramon Llull) on the EU project Light4Lungs: singlet oxygen inside bacteria, with photodiodes and photomultipliers.

Then two years at Sensofar (optical metrology) as Key Account Manager and Product Specialist for the UK, Ireland, Italy, Poland, Australia and South-East Europe. There I designed my first AI system for real users: an internal LLM assistant over a Python document database, answering technical questions from our documentation.

07 · NOW

Building AI systems, and measuring them

The way I learned to build experiments: a hypothesis, a control and a number at the end. A local assistant on my laptop, an agent that operates a lab bench, a chess reviewer that cannot invent tactics.
