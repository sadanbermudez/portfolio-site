LabDesk: a support assistant that answers only from the docs

A lab-automation team asked for PyVISA answers they could trust. Testable requirements, BM25 retrieval with checked citations, a refusal rule, a read-only power-meter tool, a FastAPI service with telemetry and a handover runbook. The held-out set, written as support tickets, was committed before the system saw it; the system misses the correctness target by one answer, and the report says why.
