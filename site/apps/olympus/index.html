Olympus: a fitness app where every number has a source

My own app for training, food and health on Android. It plans lifting and running, logs workouts and meals, reads my watch through Health Connect, and has a coach that runs on the phone. It works offline, keeps health data encrypted on the device, and every figure it shows names the formula or dataset it came from.

    How it's built

    Architecture

    Flutter, Material 3, Riverpod 3
    State lives in Riverpod providers. The theme is seeded from deep Aegean blue (#2A5BA8), light and dark, and takes the wallpaper colours on Android 12 and later.
    go_router
    Five tabs (Today, Train, Eat, Coach, Profile) in a shell route, with deep links into sessions, races and foods from widgets and notifications.
    Drift on encrypted SQLite
    66 tables on SQLite3MultipleCiphers with FTS5 search. The app refuses to open the database if the cipher is missing, and backups are re-keyed with a passphrase.
    Pure-Dart domain engines
    Lifting plans, progressive overload, recovery, VDOT paces, race prediction, nutrition targets, caffeine and readiness are plain Dart with no Flutter imports, so they are unit tested on their own. This page runs JavaScript ports of them.
    Local coach with a number guard
    Qwen3.5 2B (Q4_K_M) through llama.cpp, downloaded once and checked by SHA-256. It reads data through 18 app tools, and the guard flags any number in a reply that no tool returned.
    Platform channels
    Kotlin behind method channels: Health Connect for sleep, heart rate, HRV and steps; a foreground service that keeps GPS runs going with the screen off; and safety texts by SMS on outdoor outings.

    Principles

    • Offline first. Every core feature works in airplane mode. The network is only for sync, weather and dataset updates.
    • Every number has a source. Calories and macros come from bundled food datasets; everything else from a published formula, cited on the screen that shows it. Where the app uses its own rule, it says so.
    • Health data stays encrypted on the device. Nothing leaves the phone without an explicit toggle.
    • The engine decides, the model explains. Plans and adaptations come from deterministic code; the language model only explains them and turns free text into data.

    What this demo cannot show

    Camera rep counter
    ML Kit pose detection follows the joints on the camera feed and counts reps of a set.
    Hands-free logging
    Say "80 kilos, 8 reps" or "ochenta kilos, ocho repeticiones" after a set; a deterministic parser logs it, with no model involved.
    GPS runs
    Guided runs with spoken cues, interval paces adjusted for the heat, and the route recorded by a foreground service.
    Barcode and label scanning
    Scan a product's barcode, or read a nutrition label with on-device text recognition when the product is not in the catalogue.

    About this page

    The ported engines are tested against the values the Dart code gives (VDOT 49.8 from a 20-minute 5K, as in Daniels' tables). The exercise names, foods and nutrition values here are a small list written for this page, not the app's licensed datasets. The coach is a scripted replay; the number check on its replies is the real logic, ported.
